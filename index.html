Gastroenterology

Gut & Liver Specialist Services in Pune

Expert diagnosis and management of digestive, liver and pancreatic conditions by Dr. Vikas R. Bharti — a trained Interventional Endoscopist with DrNB (Medical Gastro).

Upper GI Endoscopy

Diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopy for esophageal, stomach, and duodenal disorders such as dyspepsia, varices, strictures, ulcers, polyps, Cancer Screening and bleeding disorders.

Colonoscopy

Complete large intestine evaluation for colon cancer screening, polyp detection & removal, rectal bleeding, hemorrhoids, ulcers and diagnosis of IBS and IBD.

ERCP

Endoscopic Retrograde Cholangiopancreatography for bile duct stones, strictures, biliary obstruction due to cancers and pancreatic disorders.

GERD Treatment

Complete evaluation and management of gastroesophageal reflux disease, hiatal hernia, and chronic heartburn disorders.

IBS & IBD Care

Expert treatment of Irritable Bowel Syndrome and Inflammatory Bowel Disease including Crohn's disease and Ulcerative Colitis.

Liver Disease

Diagnosis and management of fatty liver, hepatitis, cirrhosis, jaundice and liver function disorders by an experienced Hepatologist.

Pancreatic Disorders

Management of acute and chronic pancreatitis, pancreatic cysts and tumors with EUS-guided precision.

Gallbladder Diseases

Evaluation and management of gallstones, cholecystitis, biliary dyskinesia and related conditions with modern endoscopic techniques.

Vaccination

Vaccination in Chronic liver diseases and hepatitis A,B and E.

Gynecology & Obstetrics

Women's Health & Maternity Services in Pune

From preconception planning to postmenopause care, Dr. Swarada J. Kulkarni provides compassionate, evidence-based women's healthcare with expertise in laparoscopic surgery and reproductive medicine.

Pregnancy Care

Complete antenatal care, genetic screening,nutrition advice, Yoga Advice, normal and caesarean deliveries with mother and baby-centric birth planning.

High Risk Pregnancy

Specialised management of hypertension, gestational diabetes, twins, preterm labour and other high-risk obstetric conditions.

Laparoscopic Surgery

Minimally invasive procedures for ovarian cysts, fibroids, endometriosis, ectopic pregnancy and pelvic adhesions.

PCOS/ PCOD Treatment

Individualised treatment for Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome including hormonal management, lifestyle counselling and fertility support.

Infertility Treatment

Comprehensive infertility evaluation, ovulation induction, IUI and coordination of assisted reproductive techniques for both partners.

Menstrual Disorders

Diagnosis and treatment of irregular periods, heavy bleeding, endometriosis, fibroids and premenstrual syndrome.

Menopause Care

Holistic management of perimenopause and menopause symptoms including hormone therapy, bone health and quality of life care.

Cervical Cancer Screening & Vaccination

Pap smear, HPV testing, colposcopy and precancerous lesion management for early detection and prevention of cervical cancer.

Family Planning

Premarital and Pre-conceptional counselling, contraception advice and Medical Termination of Pregnancy (abortion) services.

Lactation Professional

Antenetal guidance and post partum support for lactation.
